# Code of Conduct

EvolveEngine is built by and for people who make games, from first-time hobbyists to professionals. Everyone who
takes part in this project (issues, pull requests, discussions, anywhere the project is represented) is expected to
help keep it a place where people want to stay and learn.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Everybody started somewhere.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Assume good intent, and say clearly when something is unclear.
- Give feedback that helps: say what to change and why. Take feedback the same way.
- Respect other people's time, work and privacy. Do not share someone's personal information.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments, including about someone's background, identity, or ability.
- Sexualised language or attention, and unwelcome physical contact or persistent unwanted messages.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing of discussions, and spam.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you see or experience behaviour like this, tell the project maintainers privately: send a message to the repository
owner (the contact details are on their profile) rather than posting in public. Reports are treated confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily
restrict, or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour harms the community. They explain their decision when it is
appropriate to do so.
